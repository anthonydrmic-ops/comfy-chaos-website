SlideSure

Waterslide safety and competency program

Online training for waterslide operators and their teams. Staff work through the modules at their own pace, pass the knowledge assessments and earn a certificate on completion.

For
Waterslide operators and their teams
Covers
Inspections, surface management, operations, defect recognition, water quality, incident prevention and emergency response
Includes
9 modules, 31 lessons, 200+ assessment questions, an 80% pass mark and a free manager dashboard

FlumeSight

Waterslide inspection and maintenance system

The daily inspection is walked part by part on a phone, and every defect is pinned to its exact spot on a 3D model of the slide. Defects are ranked by risk on a 5 by 5 matrix and roll into a 10 year capital plan.

For
Aquatic venues with waterslides and splash parks
Works on
Phones on the tower, iPads on the pool deck and the manager's desk
Status
In its launch build, with a working demo

FrontFoot

Occupational violence and aggression self-assessment for venues

Venue managers answer 39 yes or no questions in about ten minutes and get an instant control coverage score and a list of gaps. A written prevention plan follows, tailored to the venue and its state, with prioritised actions over 30, 90 and 180 days.

For
Pools, leisure centres, pubs, clubs and councils
Covers
Every Australian state and territory, plus the Commonwealth scheme
Scope
A self-assessment, not a certified audit or legal advice

WellSafe

Risk assessment for wellness operators

A risk and safety navigator for wellness operators. Choose the services you run, rate your controls and get a scored report with the gaps to close.

For
Saunas, ice baths, float tanks, steam rooms and more
Method
Each risk scored likelihood by consequence on a 5 by 5 matrix
